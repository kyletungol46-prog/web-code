Welcome to the Milky Way

Our home galaxy is a swirling collection of more than 100 billion stars, dust, gas, and at least eight planets we call neighbours. Let's explore it together!

Meet the planets

About the Milky Way

The Milky Way is a barred spiral galaxy about 100,000 light-years across. Our Solar System sits roughly two-thirds of the way out from the centre, on a quiet spiral arm called the Orion Arm. Everything you can see in the night sky with the naked eye lives inside this galaxy.
