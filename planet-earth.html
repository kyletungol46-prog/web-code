Earth, Our Home Planet

Earth is the only planet we know of that supports life, with liquid water oceans and a protective atmosphere.

Quick Facts

  • Diameter: 12,742 km
  • Moons: 1 (the Moon)
  • Day length: 24 hours
  • Year length: 365.25 days

A Living World

About 71% of Earth's surface is covered by water. Its atmosphere of nitrogen and oxygen, along with a magnetic field, shields life from harmful solar radiation.
