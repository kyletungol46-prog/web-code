Jupiter, King of the Planets

Jupiter is the largest planet in our Solar System. You could fit more than 1,300 Earths inside it!

Quick Facts

  • Diameter: 139,820 km
  • Moons: At least 95 known
  • Day length: About 10 Earth-hours
  • Year length: About 12 Earth-years

The Great Red Spot

Jupiter's most famous feature is a giant storm called the Great Red Spot. It is more than twice as wide as Earth and has been swirling for at least 350 years.
