Mars, The Red Planet

Mars gets its red colour from rusty iron-rich dust that covers its surface.

Quick Facts

  • Diameter: 6,779 km
  • Moons: 2 (Phobos and Deimos)
  • Day length: About 24.6 Earth-hours
  • Year length: About 687 Earth-days

Olympus Mons

Mars is home to the largest volcano in the Solar System, Olympus Mons, which stands about 22 km tall — nearly three times the height of Mount Everest.
