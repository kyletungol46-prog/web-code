Venus, Earth's Twin

Venus is similar in size to Earth, but it is the hottest planet in our Solar System.

Quick Facts

  • Diameter: 12,104 km
  • Moons: 0
  • Day length: About 243 Earth-days
  • Year length: About 225 Earth-days

A Runaway Greenhouse

Venus is wrapped in thick clouds of carbon dioxide and sulfuric acid. These trap heat, pushing surface temperatures to around 465°C — hot enough to melt lead.
